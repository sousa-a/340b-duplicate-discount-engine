# Generator truth (WORLD 1)

**All data in this folder is completely synthetic.** No record describes a real covered entity, pharmacy,
prescriber, patient or wholesaler. Prices are in US dollars as a proof of concept of a working engine: apart from
the CMS-published MFPs and the manufacturer-set 2023 list prices used as starting points, every price and amount
is synthetic and does not reflect what any manufacturer charges, reports or pays. See SYNTHETIC_DATA_NOTICE.md
in the repository.

Generator records with truth columns. Only the evaluation step reads this folder; the engine never does.
